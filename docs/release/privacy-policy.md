_Last updated: [date]_

UniCebim ("the app") is a budgeting app for students and young people. It is operated by
[name / company], [address], contact: [contact email] ("we").

## What we collect

| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| E-mail address and password (stored only as a hash) | Your account, sign-in, password reset | Supabase Auth |
| Name | Shown in the app | Supabase database |
| Profile photo (optional) | Shown in the app | Supabase Storage (public link that is hard to guess) |
| Wallets, transactions, payments, subscriptions, fixed expenses, budget settings | The app's features | Supabase database |
| Currency, time zone and language (from your device at sign-up) | Correct amounts and dates | Supabase database |
| Bank statement files you choose to import | Read on your device only; the file itself is never uploaded, only the transactions you confirm | Your device |

We do **not** use advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs, do not sell your data and do not share it
for marketing. We do not access your contacts, location or camera.

## Service providers

- **Supabase** (database, authentication, file storage) hosts your data in the EU (Ireland) and acts as
  our processor.
- **unavatar.io**: to show a subscription's logo, the app requests a logo for a web address guessed from
  the subscription name (for example `spotify.com`). The request comes from your device, so the
  provider sees your IP address and that address; your account data is not sent.
- **Apple / Google** deliver the app and, if you enable them, run on-device features such as Face ID
  / fingerprint (the app never receives biometric data) and local notifications.

## On your device

The app keeps a copy of your recent data on the device so it works offline, plus your sign-in session
and device settings (reminders, app lock). Reminders are scheduled locally; no server sends them.
Signing out removes this data from the device.

## How long we keep data

As long as you have an account. **Deleting your account** (Profile → Delete account) permanently
removes your account, all your financial records and your profile photo immediately. Backups kept by
our provider expire within [backup retention, e.g. 7 days].

## Your rights

You can see and correct your data in the app, export your transactions (Statistics → Create and share
a report) and delete your account at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under the
GDPR or Turkey's KVKK) you may also ask us for access, correction, deletion, restriction or
portability, and you may complain to your data protection authority. Contact: [contact email].

## Children

UniCebim is not intended for children under [13 / 16 depending on the region]. 

## Changes

We will announce material changes in the app before they take effect.

## Hizmet sağlayıcılar

- **Supabase** (veritabanı, kimlik doğrulama, dosya depolama) verini AB'de (İrlanda) barındırır ve veri
  işleyen olarak çalışır.
- **unavatar.io**: Abonelik logosunu göstermek için uygulama, abonelik adından tahmin edilen bir web
  adresinin (örn. `spotify.com`) logosunu ister. İstek cihazından gider; sağlayıcı IP adresini ve bu
  adresi görür, hesap verilerin gönderilmez.
- **Apple / Google** uygulamayı dağıtır ve açarsan Face ID / parmak izi (biyometrik veri uygulamaya
  hiç gelmez) ile yerel bildirimleri cihazda çalıştırır.

## Cihazında tutulanlar

Uygulama çevrimdışı çalışabilmek için son verilerinin bir kopyasını, oturumunu ve cihaz ayarlarını
(hatırlatmalar, uygulama kilidi) cihazda tutar. Hatırlatmalar cihazda planlanır, sunucu göndermez.
Çıkış yapınca bunlar cihazdan silinir.

## Saklama süresi

Hesabın olduğu sürece. **Hesabı silmek** (Profil → Hesabı sil) hesabını, tüm finansal kayıtlarını ve
profil fotoğrafını hemen ve kalıcı olarak siler. Sağlayıcımızdaki yedekler [yedek saklama süresi, örn.
7 gün] içinde sona erer.
