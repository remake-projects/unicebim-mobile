## English

- **Name:** UniCebim: Student Budget
- **Subtitle (iOS):** Spending, bills & daily limit
- **Short description (Play):** Know what you can spend today. Wallets, bills and subscriptions in one place.
- **Keywords (iOS):** budget,student,expense,tracker,money,allowance,scholarship,bills,subscription,savings,wallet
- **Category:** Finance
- **Full description:**

  UniCebim is a budget app made for students and young people. Instead of a wall of charts, it answers
  one question every day: how much can I spend today?

  • Spendable money: your wallets minus the payments you still owe
  • Daily limit that accounts for rent, bills and the days left in the month
  • Wallets for cash, bank cards or a campus card, with transfers between them
  • Upcoming payments with a warning when your balance won't cover them, and one-tap "paid"
  • Subscriptions with logos and a monthly total
  • Monthly and 6-month statistics, plus a CSV report you can share
  • Import Excel statements from Ziraat Bankası and İş Bankası
  • Bill splitter for dinners and shared flats
  • Reminders the day before payments and renewals, and on your income day
  • Optional Face ID / fingerprint lock; works offline with your last data

  No ads, no tracking. Delete your account and all your data from the app at any time.
